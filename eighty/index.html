Riders are sorted by live position from every timing point around the course. Tap Leader through each time the leader passes the 80% zone (about timing checkpoint 13): riders who haven't reached checkpoint 13 yet are as far behind as the timer; riders past it show their gap at checkpoint 13. Live gap follows timing round the course, with ▲ time lost or ▼ gained since checkpoint 13. Yellow: past 50% of the leader's first lap. Red: past 75%. PULL: past 80% (the pull time). Orange: losing more time than on their previous lap (crash or mechanical?). Pulsing blue: lapped, not at checkpoint 13 since your previous Leader through tap, so look for them. Under each name: expected arrival at the 80% zone from the rider's earlier laps and current pace, and a forecast: PULL LIKELY if their projected gap there is past the pull time, Possible pull if past 75% or heading there. SAFE: took the bell (crossed the line after the leader started the last lap) or the leader has finished; no more pulls for them. Pulled only changes how a rider is shown.
